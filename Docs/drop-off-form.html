Donate Furniture —
Get Your Tax Receipt

Dropping off your furniture donation yourself? Complete this form to register it with Furniture Assist and receive your official tax receipt.

Drop-off hours: Sundays, 10am–2pm Check the calendar for this Sunday's drop-off, and confirm your items are on the accepted items list before you come.

Before You Submit

  1. Confirm your items are on our accepted items list. Only items on the list qualify as donations to Furniture Assist.
  2. Use the date you plan to drop off as the Donation Date — this is the date that appears on your tax receipt.
  3. After submitting, you'll get a confirmation email with a QR code — bring it (on your phone or printed) when you drop off, so we can check you in quickly.
  4. Your PDF tax receipt will be emailed once our team has processed your donation.

What Happens Next

Here's what to expect.

Right Now

Confirmation Email With Your QR Code

Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a confirmation email with a QR code. Bring it — on your phone or printed — when you drop off, so we can check you in quickly. The code doesn't expire; if you can't find it when you arrive, just let us know and we'll look you up.

Sunday, 10am–2pm

Drop Off Your Items

Bring your donation during drop-off hours. Show your QR code at check-in.

After Drop-Off

PDF Tax Receipt Emailed to You

Once processed, your official Furniture Assist tax receipt will be emailed to you as a PDF. Keep it for your records.

Reminder: Furniture Assist does not provide valuations for donated items. The fair market value of your donation for tax purposes is your responsibility to determine. Questions? Contact us at info@furnitureassist.com or 973-868-6007 (landline only — no texts).
